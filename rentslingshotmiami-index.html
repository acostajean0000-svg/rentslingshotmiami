What is a Polaris Slingshot?

The Most Thrilling Ride in Miami

The Polaris Slingshot is a 3-wheeled open-air autocycle with a powerful 2.0L engine, sports car agility, and absolutely no roof — putting you right in the heart of Miami's streets, wind, and sunshine. It's not a car. It's not a motorcycle. It's something entirely different.

In Florida, the Slingshot is classified as a motorcycle but does not require a motorcycle endorsement — just a valid driver's license. All models come with Bluetooth audio, safety harnesses, and a premium sound system to soundtrack your Miami cruise.
