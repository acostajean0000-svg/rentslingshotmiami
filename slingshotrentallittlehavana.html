Little Havana, Miami

Experience Calle Ocho From an Open Cockpit

There's no part of Miami more alive than Little Havana. SW 8th Street — Calle Ocho — is a continuous outdoor party: dominoes at Maximo Gomez Park, handmade cigars rolled in storefront windows, Cuban coffee served through walk-up counters, and salsa bands spilling out of open restaurant doors.

Now imagine experiencing all of it from the open cockpit of a Polaris Slingshot — windows nonexistent, engine rumbling, the rhythm of the neighborhood washing over you at every red light. This isn't a tour bus window. This is full immersion.

From our North Miami Beach pickup, Little Havana is about 30 minutes south. Add Brickell and Downtown to your loop and you've got one of the best half-day routes in all of Miami — culture, skyline, and coastline in a single ride.

🎺
Local tip: If your visit falls on the last Friday of the month, you've hit the jackpot. Viernes Culturales (Cultural Fridays) turns Calle Ocho into a street festival with live music, art galleries, and food vendors. The Slingshot becomes the star of the street — everyone wants a photo.
What You'll See

Top Little Havana Slingshot Stops

🎲
Maximo Gomez Park
Known as Domino Park — the legendary spot where Cuban exiles have played dominos under the shade trees for decades. Park the Slingshot nearby and watch the masters play.
🛣️
Calle Ocho Strip
The heartbeat of Little Havana. A slow cruise down SW 8th Street takes you past murals, markets, music venues, and some of Miami's most authentic Cuban restaurants.
⚾
Marlins Park
loanDepot park — the Miami Marlins' home stadium — sits right at the edge of Little Havana. Its dramatic architecture makes a striking Slingshot backdrop on game days and off.
🎭
Tower Theater
A 1926 Art Deco landmark on Calle Ocho that has been a cultural anchor for the Cuban-American community. Recognized on the National Register of Historic Places.
🌆
Brickell Skyline
A 5-minute drive from Little Havana, Brickell's glass towers create one of the most dramatic skylines in the Southeast. Day or night, the contrast with Little Havana is stunning.
🌿
Jose Marti Park
A riverside green space at the southern edge of Little Havana, named after Cuba's national hero. Great rest stop with river views before heading toward Brickell or Coconut Grove.
Recommended Route

The Little Havana Culture & Skyline Loop

📍 North Miami Beach → Calle Ocho → Brickell → Back (3–4 hrs)

  1. 1
    Pickup — 1550 NE 168th St, North Miami Beach. Head south on I-95 — the fastest route through the heart of Miami. Exit at SW 8th St / Calle Ocho.
  2. 2
    Calle Ocho cruise. Roll west on SW 8th Street, taking in murals, markets, and cafecito windows. Stop at Maximo Gomez Park to watch the domino masters at work.
  3. 3
    loanDepot park loop. Swing past the Marlins stadium on NW 16th Ave for a dramatic architecture stop before heading toward Downtown.
  4. 4
    Downtown Miami → Brickell Bridge. Cross the Miami River on the Brickell Bridge and get an instant reveal of the glass canyon ahead.
  5. 5
    Brickell Avenue skyline cruise. Head south on Brickell Ave with towers flanking both sides. Turn around at Mary Brickell Village and head toward Bayfront Park.
  6. 6
    Bayside Marketplace → MacArthur Causeway. Wind through Downtown, hit Bayside for waterfront views, then take the MacArthur Causeway with open bay on both sides back toward North Miami Beach.
